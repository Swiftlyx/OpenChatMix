# OpenChatMix 2.0.0

The second major release. OpenChatMix now processes your microphone, manages output devices, gives
Game and Chat their own volume, and has a new panel built on Material 3 Expressive. Virtual device
mode no longer crackles.

## Highlights

- **Microphone chain.** Noise suppression (RNNoise, Standard or Strong with a voice gate), a
  compressor, a 10-band equalizer with voice presets and your own VST 2 and VST 3 plugins, played into
  a virtual cable that Discord, OBS or a game use as their microphone, like LightHost with the effects
  built in. Drag the steps to change their order, listen to the result on your headphones, and keep
  the microphone and cable levels at 100%. A hotkey switches it to the next microphone.
- **Output devices.** Put the connected devices in your order of preference. The sound can move to
  the next one when the output disconnects and back when the preferred one returns, and a hotkey sends
  it to the next device.
- **Game and Chat volume.** Each side has its own volume on top of ChatMix, with a live meter in its
  slider. ChatMix no longer moves the app sliders of the Windows volume mixer, so volumes you set for
  apps yourself keep working.
- **Virtual device mode without crackling.** The bridges keep enough audio ahead and absorb the clock
  difference between devices by playing a hair faster or slower, instead of cutting audio. Sound after
  silence starts at the right pitch, and the taskbar slider and volume keys work, while recordings of
  the Game and Chat devices keep the full level.
- **A new panel.** Material 3 Expressive throughout: groups of tiles, connected button groups,
  dropdowns, spring motion and values you can type. Soft, pastel colors, a color of its own for Chat,
  standard, medium or high contrast, and the whole panel works from the keyboard.
- **More ways to set the balance.** Hold a key combination and turn the mouse wheel. The tray icon
  can show its own mark at the even balance.
- **Headset presets in place.** Saved presets on the *Headset* tab can be applied, renamed, copied and
  deleted right in the equalizer card.
- **Battery on more headsets.** The Arctis Nova 7, 7 Gen 2, 7X, 7+ and Nova 5X now report their
  battery level. It shows beside the app's name on the *Mixer* tab, the tray icon can show it at the
  even balance, in the theme's color or from green to red by its charge, and a popup warns when it runs
  low.
- **A short tour** on the first start, and output devices you never want to switch to.
- **Export and import of all settings** in one ZIP file, with a backup of the previous ones on import.
- **How the headset is connected** (USB, 2.4 GHz, Bluetooth, 3.5 mm) beside its name on the *Mixer* tab.
- **Virtual device mode per channel.** Game, Chat and Media each choose where they play, and Media
  can have a virtual device and an equalizer of its own.
- **Material 3 at the tray icon**: a hover card and a right-click menu, plus tooltips in the panel.
- **A visualizer on the GameDAC screen**: bars across the whole screen while music or a video plays.
- **3rd party add-ons for the GameDAC screen**: GameSense programs you already have, such as the Discord
  program that comes with SteelSeries GG, show on the GameDAC while GG is closed, each Discord event with
  a screen you set up as in GG's Engine.
- **Bluetooth on the screensaver** (Nova Pro Wireless): an icon in a corner while the base station's
  Bluetooth is on, blinking while it pairs.
- **GameDAC line out**: speakers or a stream mix with its own levels, and a hotkey for the screensaver,
  which now also runs next to SteelSeries GG and gives way to GG's Discord, Now Playing and visualizer
  while they show something.
- **Smaller things.** The tray icon morphs between the bars and the balanced mark. Apps can be
  removed from a group (requested by [@zer0ed](https://github.com/zer0ed) in [#3](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/issues/3)), and the Xbox party chat starts in the Chat
  group (suggested by [@zer0ed](https://github.com/zer0ed) in [#4](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/issues/4)); default chat apps are listed only once installed.
- A Ukrainian README.

OpenChatMix itself still needs only about 4 MB of memory; the microphone chain, the equalizers and VST
plugins add what they need on top (a neural-network plugin can take hundreds of megabytes).

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Upgrading from 1.0.0

- 1.0.0 finds this version on its own once a day (*Settings → Updates*), downloads it, checks it
  against `SHA256SUMS.txt` and restarts. You can also replace `OpenChatMix.exe` by hand.
- Your settings carry over. Two things are gone: the *Headphones* screensaver animation (a saved
  choice of it becomes *Off*) and the *Next EQ preset* hotkey.
- Colors are softer now: a vivid accent or custom color is shown as a pastel.
- New installs start with the Media group and the equalizer off; an upgrade keeps your choices.

## Files

- `OpenChatMix.exe`: the app, portable.
- `OpenChatMix-2.0.0-win-x64.zip`: the app with both READMEs, the license and the third-party notices.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums.

The binary is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask for confirmation on the first run, and some
antivirus programs may flag a new, unsigned program; these are false positives. Compare the file with
`SHA256SUMS.txt`, read the source code or build it yourself. Third-party code (RNNoise, the VST 3 SDK
interfaces) is listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
