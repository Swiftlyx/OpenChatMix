# OpenChatMix 2.5.8

The Nova Elite's microphone gets its own equalizer, in the headset itself.

## Highlights

- **Microphone equalizer on the Nova Elite (experimental).** The switch above the equalizer on the *Headset*
  tab now has a third choice, *Microphone*: GG's Balanced, Broadcast High Pitch, Clarity High Pitch and Deep
  Voice presets, Flat and ten band sliders for your own curve. It works in the headset itself, so unlike the
  microphone equalizer on the *Microphone* page it needs no virtual cable and adds no latency.
- **Bluetooth equalizer confirmed.** The Elite's equalizer for sound over Bluetooth from 2.5.7 works.

Both thanks to [@Lasfecas](https://github.com/Lasfecas) in [#6](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/issues/6): the presets match what GG sends in their USB capture. If the
microphone equalizer doesn't change your voice, please tell us there.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Upgrading

- Earlier versions find this one on their own once a day (*Settings → Updates*), download it, check it
  against `SHA256SUMS.txt` and restart. You can also replace `OpenChatMix.exe` by hand.
- Your settings carry over.

## Files

- `OpenChatMix.exe`: the app, portable.
- `OpenChatMix-2.5.8-win-x64.zip`: the app with both READMEs, the license and the third-party notices.
- `DeepFilterNet-OpenChatMix.dll`: the optional AI noise suppression plugin, built from `tools/deepfilter`.
- `DeepFilterNet-NOTICES.txt`: the licenses of DeepFilterNet and the Rust crates inside the plugin.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums.

The binaries are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask for confirmation on the first run, and some
antivirus programs may flag a new, unsigned program; these are false positives. Compare the files with
`SHA256SUMS.txt`, read the source code or build them yourself. Third-party code (RNNoise, the VST 3 SDK
interfaces, DeepFilterNet in the plugin) is listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
