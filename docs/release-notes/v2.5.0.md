# OpenChatMix 2.5.0

NVIDIA noise removal, a log switch, popups for the GameDAC knob and more, mostly from the ideas in [#6](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/issues/6).

## Highlights

- **NVIDIA noise removal for RTX cards.** OpenChatMix can use NVIDIA's own Audio Effects SDK (the models
  behind NVIDIA Broadcast) as a plugin, without the Broadcast app:
  1. Install the Audio Effects redistributable for your card from [NVIDIA's SDK page](https://www.nvidia.com/broadcast-sdk-resources).
  2. *Settings → Microphone → Plugins → Add plugin* → `NVAudioEffects.dll` in `C:\Program Files\NVIDIA Corporation\NVIDIA Audio Effects SDK`.
  3. Below the plugin list pick *Noise*, *Room echo* or *Both* and the strength.

  The model loads on the graphics card in the background; the card below the list shows when it is
  ready. It also works on a channel of virtual device mode at 48 kHz. Requested by [@liquidshadowfoxx](https://github.com/liquidshadowfoxx).
- **The log in Settings.** *Settings → General → Diagnostics → Write a log* turns it on at once, without
  editing the settings file, and *Open the log folder* shows it. Requested by [@liquidshadowfoxx](https://github.com/liquidshadowfoxx).
- **Popups for the GameDAC knob.** Turning the knob of a GameDAC or base station now shows the headset
  volume in volume mode and the balance in ChatMix mode. *Settings → Notifications* has a switch for
  each, plus one switch for all popups and the corner of the screen they show in. Settings for hardware
  your headset does not have are no longer shown. Requested by [@liquidshadowfoxx](https://github.com/liquidshadowfoxx).
- **Spare battery.** The Nova Elite's spare battery in the base station shows next to the headset's on
  the *Headset* tab and in the tray. Requested by [@liquidshadowfoxx](https://github.com/liquidshadowfoxx).
- **Fewer pops on the Nova 7 Gen 2.** The equalizer is saved to the headset once you stop changing it,
  so clicking through presets pops once instead of on every click. Reported by [@zer0ed](https://github.com/zer0ed) in [#7](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/issues/7).
- **Confirmed.** The Gen 2 equalizer and the output switching with the headset's power work on the
  Nova 7 and the Nova 7 Gen 2 ([@zer0ed](https://github.com/zer0ed), [#7](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/issues/7)); the Nova Elite's settings and output switching work too
  ([@liquidshadowfoxx](https://github.com/liquidshadowfoxx), [#6](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/issues/6)).

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Swiftlyx/OpenChatMix/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Upgrading

- Earlier versions find this one on their own once a day (*Settings → Updates*), download it, check it
  against `SHA256SUMS.txt` and restart. You can also replace `OpenChatMix.exe` by hand.
- Your settings carry over.

## Files

- `OpenChatMix.exe`: the app, portable.
- `OpenChatMix-2.5.0-win-x64.zip`: the app with both READMEs, the license and the third-party notices.
- `DeepFilterNet-OpenChatMix.dll`: the optional AI noise suppression plugin, built from `tools/deepfilter`.
- `DeepFilterNet-NOTICES.txt`: the licenses of DeepFilterNet and the Rust crates inside the plugin.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums.

The binaries are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask for confirmation on the first run, and some
antivirus programs may flag a new, unsigned program; these are false positives. Compare the files with
`SHA256SUMS.txt`, read the source code or build them yourself. Third-party code (RNNoise, the VST 3 SDK
interfaces, DeepFilterNet in the plugin) is listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
